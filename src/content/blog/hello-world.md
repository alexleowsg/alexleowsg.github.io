It may mean the service itself is not fully standardised yet.

Or it may mean the fulfilment teams do not have enough capacity, authority, or information to support what the service is promising.

A request form gives users a place to submit.

A workflow gives the ticket somewhere to go.

But neither of those guarantees that the service can be fulfilled properly.

## My Reminder For Future Service Design

Make the common cases clear and doable.

Make the difficult cases visible and give them an owner.

Do not depend on one or two individuals to keep the entire process moving.

A good service catalogue item is not just a form. It is a promise that the organisation knows how to fulfil.
